## The golden position

- The verb **always** sits in **second position**, no exceptions.
- Subject and object can swap places around the verb; the case articles show who does what.

| | |
|---|---|
| Der Hund fängt den Ball. | The dog catches the ball. |
| Den Ball fängt der Hund. | The dog catches the ball. (same meaning) |

- Three required parts: **subject + verb + object**.
